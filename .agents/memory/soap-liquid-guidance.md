---
name: Soap liquid guidance
description: Rationale and safety limits for automatic cold-process liquid and fragrance estimates
---

Use a disclosed 2:1 mixing-liquid-to-NaOH mass baseline for ordinary cold-process calculations, not a claim that the amount is optimized for each formula. Fragrance is part of batch mass, not an input to oil saponification or the NaOH amount.

**Why:** SAP values describe oil alkali demand, not the ideal process liquid for a specific maker, liquid substitute, or fragrance. A simple automatic starting point meets the request to remove the concentration control without inventing precision or supplier-specific safety limits.

**How to apply:** Keep the assumption visible wherever liquid is shown. Milk or other liquids may need special temperature and formulation handling; fragrance usage needs the specific supplier's applicable limit. If replacing the baseline with formula-specific advice, first obtain the missing validated process data. Existing saved formulas with a concentration setting must warn when recalculated under a different liquid rule.
