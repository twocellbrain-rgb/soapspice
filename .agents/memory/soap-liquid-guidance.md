---
name: Soap liquid guidance
description: Rationale and safety limits for automatic cold-process liquid and fragrance estimates
---

Use a disclosed 22%-of-complete-pre-saponification-batch liquid estimate, not a claim that the amount is optimized for each formula or that it reproduces Bramble Berry's proprietary calculation. The total includes oils, NaOH, fragrance, and liquid itself; it is not 22% of oil weight. Fragrance affects the batch-based liquid estimate but not oil saponification or the NaOH amount. Keep the 100% NaOH purity assumption visible, not Bramble Berry's 97% assumption.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose this approximation over the prior 2:1 baseline, kept the 100% purity assumption, and declined extra controls. Bramble Berry publicly mentions ~22% liquid in a recipe but does not document "22% of oil weight" or the exact calculator algorithm; using oil weight alone risks too little liquid for high-SAP oils. SAP values still cannot establish an ideal process liquid for a particular recipe.

**How to apply:** Solve liquid as 0.22 / 0.78 times the mass of oils + NaOH + fragrance; show it as a starting estimate, never as verified optimal or an exact vendor formula. Do not add a concentration or discount control unless newly requested. Milk or other liquids may need special handling; fragrance usage needs supplier-specific limits. Warn when older saved formulas used a different liquid rule.
