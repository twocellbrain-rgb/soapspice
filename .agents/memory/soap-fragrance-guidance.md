For SoapSpice fragrance guidance, use Bramble Berry's published 0.7 oz per pound of total cold-process soap as the general medium-strength reference. Clearly identify other guide strengths as SoapSpice suggestions, not Bramble Berry's fragrance-specific rates. A fragrance-specific safety screen requires the supplier's IFRA maximum for that exact fragrance and bar-soap application; do not infer one from a scent name or from strength guidance. When cured weight is unavailable, using oil weight for the supplier's finished-product percentage is a conservative screening approximation, never a compliance certificate. The combined scent-strength dose belongs to one batch; individual fragrance IFRA screens do not establish safety for a blend with shared restricted ingredients.

**Why:** The user directed us to use Bramble Berry's calculator as a reference, but published general scent guidance and supplier IFRA finished-product limits answer different questions. Cured-bar mass and restricted constituents of blended fragrances are not known to this calculator.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction whenever changing fragrance calculations, copy, exports, or saved recipes. Show missing supplier rates as unverified and do not imply that independently passing fragrance limits makes a blend compliant.
